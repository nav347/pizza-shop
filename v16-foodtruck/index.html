The story

One second-hand van, one borrowed oven, one stubborn idea: pizza should taste like a street festival at 11 pm, loud, hot and shared.

Dough ferments 48 hours, gets stretched by hand while you watch, and bakes in a wood-fired oven at 450 degrees. No white tablecloths, just a line of people who know they are in the right place.
