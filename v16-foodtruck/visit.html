Quick questions

Do you deliver on apps?

Yes, find us on the usual delivery apps within about 5 km of the truck. Ordering direct online or by phone is faster and cheaper for you.

Where do I park?

Pay-and-display street parking nearby (read the signs, Toronto is strict) plus a Green P lot a short walk away. Bike racks right by the truck.

Do you do catering?

Yes. Offices, weddings, birthdays, condo events: 15 pizzas or more, give us a week's notice and we bring the truck and the oven.

What about winter hours?

November to March: Wed to Sat, 11:30 am to 7 pm, weather permitting. The oven keeps the queue warm, we promise.
