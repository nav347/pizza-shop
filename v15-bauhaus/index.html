04 / STORY

48 hours.
One oven.
No shortcuts.

Dough Re Mi began with one stubborn idea: pizza dough deserves patience. We ferment it for two days, stretch it by hand, and bake it in a wood-fired oven at 450 degrees.

Every sauce is made in-house each morning. Every pizza leaves the oven a little charred, a little dramatic, and entirely worth it.
