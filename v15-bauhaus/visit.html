FAQ

Do you deliver?

Yes. We are on the major delivery apps (DoorDash, Uber Eats and SkipTheDishes) within about 6 km of Oven Lane. For the hottest pizza, order online or call and we will tell you the real wait.

Is there parking?

There is metered street parking on Oven Lane and a Green P lot a two-minute walk away. We are also a short walk from the streetcar stop and have bike racks out front.

Do you do catering?

Absolutely. Office lunches, birthdays and weddings: ten pizzas or more, with 48 hours notice by phone or online. We bring boxes, napkins and the drama.
