The dust problem

Sanding drywall makes a powder finer than flour. It floats for hours, settles into vents and carpets, ruins fresh paint and ends up in lungs. Regular cleaners do not fix it, and neither does a broom.

  • 10 microns and smaller: dust that a broom just stirs up
  • Days to settle fully, then it sits on every ledge
  • 1 pass with a HEPA vacuum and damp wipe beats five with a mop
