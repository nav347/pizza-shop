Questions

How soon can you come after the drywall crew finishes?

Usually within 1 to 2 business days; rush next-day slots open up most weeks.

Do you work with the drywall company?

Yes. Many of our jobs come from drywall crews who hand the site to us on the last day.

Is the dust really gone?

We use sealed HEPA vacuums and air scrubbers, then wipe every surface. Most dust is invisible, so we check with a light.

Do I need to be home?

No. A lockbox or a key handoff is fine, and we send photos when we finish.

How is the price worked out?

By room count or square footage and how much sanding was done. Prices on this site are starting prices in CAD, before HST.

What areas do you cover?

Toronto and the GTA: Mississauga, Brampton, Vaughan, Markham, Oakville and Pickering.
