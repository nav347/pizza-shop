Homeowner FAQ

Questions from homeowners

Do you work in houses and condos, not just commercial buildings?

Yes. We take residential jobs in houses, townhomes and condos across the GTA, as well as commercial and industrial work.

Can you clean up after my renovation contractor finishes?

Yes. Post-renovation and post-drywall cleanup is one of our most requested home services. Tell us when the trades finish and we will plan around it.

Do I need to be home during the job?

Not always. For exterior work we only need access to water and the areas to be washed. For indoor work we agree access with you when we book.

How do I get a price for my home?

Choose Residential on the quote form, or call or email us with your address, the work and your timing. Photos help us quote faster.
