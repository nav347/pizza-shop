# Adarsh's version of the Maple Axis site

This folder is your own copy. Change anything in here and nothing else on the site is affected.
It started as the Maple Axis (version 11) look: near-black and gold.

Pages: index.html (home), services.html, quote.html. The look lives in style.css.

Phone, email, area and hours are NOT typed into the pages. They come from ../business.js (shared by all versions). Change them there once and every page updates.

What to change:
- Text: open the page (index.html, services.html or quote.html) and edit the words between the tags.
- Colours: top of style.css, the lines starting with --bg, --gold, --tx (hex codes like #f2b233).
- Pictures: the hero and scene pictures are drawn inside the pages (svg); logo files are in ../assets. To use your own photo, put it in this folder and point an img tag at it.

The quote form is not connected yet. Pressing the button only shows a message asking people to call or email. Orders will not arrive anywhere until we connect it.
