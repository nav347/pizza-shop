FAQ

How soon can you come after the drywallers finish?

Usually within 48 hours. Rush same-day is available when the schedule allows.

Do I need to be home?

No. A lockbox or a contractor handoff works. We send photos when done.

Is dust from sanding really a problem?

Yes. Drywall dust is extremely fine, settles into vents and floors and re-circulates for weeks if not HEPA-vacuumed.

How is the price set?

From the base price per service, adjusted by square footage and condition. You get a fixed quote before we start.

Do you work with drywall crews directly?

Yes. We have subcontract partnerships: the crew books us, we clean on their schedule, they keep the client.

Which areas do you cover?

Toronto and the GTA: Mississauga, Brampton, Vaughan, Markham, Pickering and nearby.
