Fine dust, fine finishes

Drywall dust is a quiet threat to fine millwork, stone, hardwood and upholstery. Left behind, it dulls finishes and lingers in the air for weeks.

Our team arrives in clean shoe covers, protects what you have chosen with care, and removes every trace with sealed HEPA equipment.
