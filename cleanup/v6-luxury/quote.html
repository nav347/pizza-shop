Questions

How much does a cleanup cost?

Most homes land between $350 and $1,200 depending on size and how much drywall work was done. You get a fixed quote before we start.

Do you work with my drywall contractor?

Yes. We work after the drywall crew finishes and can coordinate directly with them on timing.

Is HEPA vacuuming really needed?

Drywall dust is extremely fine. Regular vacuums pass it straight through the filter and back into the air. HEPA captures it.

Do you haul away the offcuts?

Yes, debris and offcut haul-away is one of our core services and can be added to any cleanup.

How soon can you come?

Often within 1 to 2 business days. Rush same-day visits are possible when our schedule allows.
