Questions

Do you deliver?

Yes, through the major delivery apps (DoorDash, Uber Eats, SkipTheDishes). For pickup, order online or call us.

Is there parking?

Street parking and a paid Green P lot are nearby. The streetcar stops close by.

Do you cater or take party orders?

Yes. Catering trays and party orders need 48 hours notice. Call us and we will plan it together.

Are there vegetarian or gluten-free choices?

Plenty. Look for the V, VG and GF marks on the menu.
