Our story

It started with one stubborn idea: pizza dough deserves 48 hours of patience. We ferment it slowly, stretch it by hand and bake it in a wood-fired oven.

Every sauce is made in-house each morning. Every pizza leaves the oven a little charred, a little dramatic, and completely worth the wait.
