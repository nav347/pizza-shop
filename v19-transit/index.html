About the lineOur story

Dough Re Mi started with one stubborn idea: pizza dough deserves 48 hours of patience. We ferment it slowly, stretch it by hand and bake it in a wood-fired oven.

Every sauce is made in-house each morning, and every pizza leaves the oven a little charred and completely worth the wait.
